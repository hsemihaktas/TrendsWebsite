```text
Design a [personal portfolio/nostalgia product/retro-web experience] in Web 1.0 / Webcore aesthetic.

Web 1.0 is the visual language of the early public internet — circa 1994-2004. Before professional web design was a discipline, personal websites were built by individuals who were learning HTML as they went. The result was chaotic, personal, and unmistakably human.

The signatures: tiled repeating background patterns (animated GIFs often included), beveled button borders (achieved with border-color creating 3D illusion), the default Times New Roman or Arial before web fonts existed, visitor counters, "under construction" GIFs, animated marquee text, guestbooks.

The browser: Netscape Navigator or Internet Explorer 4 chrome — classic blue-gray title bars, pixel-perfect beveled edges on everything, the Windows 95/98 desktop aesthetic.

The energy: completely earnest. No irony in the original — genuine excitement about having a presence on the internet. The nostalgia version CAN be ironic, but the best Webcore design captures the original sincerity.

Color palette: the specific blues and grays of Windows 98 UI chrome (#000080 deep blue, #c0c0c0 silver gray, #ffffff white), OR the personal expression of individual Geocities pages (anything goes — neon on black, floral on cream, blue flames on blue).

Apply this to my [project]: [describe whether you want the official browser-chrome aesthetic or the personal Geocities-page aesthetic, and what the site is about]. The result should look like it could have existed in 1999 — and should somehow be charming because of it, not despite it.
```
