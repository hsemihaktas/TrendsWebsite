## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [study app/music player/wellness platform/personal productivity tool] in Lo-fi Aesthetic.

The lo-fi aesthetic emerged from a YouTube genre of music — "lo-fi hip hop radio — beats to study/relax to" — and its iconic visual: an anime-inspired girl studying at a window while rain falls, warm lamplight, a cat sleeping, vinyl records nearby. It's become a genuine visual genre: cozy, melancholic, focused, and deeply comforting.

The visual world: a small domestic space — a study corner, a bedroom at night, a rain-soaked window — lit by warm amber lamplight. Everything is slightly blue at the edges from nighttime cool, warm amber at the center from the lamp. Analog objects: vinyl records, cassette tapes, old books, typewriters, CRT monitors.

The anime-inspired illustration style: slightly stylized characters (not fully realistic, not super-chibi), simple backgrounds with strong atmospheric perspective, warm/cool light contrast.

Color palette: night blue-purple in shadows, warm amber and orange in lamplight, occasional neon accent from a vintage electronics LED (green clock numbers, blue screen glow). Muted, slightly desaturated overall — analog film look, not digital.

Texture: VHS scanlines, film grain, slight color bleed, chromatic aberration at edges. These "imperfections" are signals of warmth and analog humanity.

Typography: a soft rounded sans-serif or a pixel font — references both the digital nostalgia and the handmade quality.

Apply this to my [app/product]: [describe what you're building — what task or feeling it supports]. The result should make users feel calm, focused, and slightly nostalgic — like they're studying in the best environment they've ever imagined.
```
