## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [environmental product/sustainability platform/community app] in Solarpunk aesthetic.

Solarpunk is a visual and philosophical movement that imagines an optimistic future where technology and nature coexist — not as opposites, but as integrated partners. It rejects both the gray concrete of dystopian sci-fi AND the sanitized tech-white of startup culture.

The visual world: abundant plant life integrated into architecture (rooftop gardens, living walls, solar-paneled greenhouses), warm golden sunlight filtering through green canopies, community spaces where people tend gardens together. The technology is visible and solar-powered — but it blends into the natural world rather than dominating it.

Color palette: rich botanical greens (not the lime-neon of tech logos, but the deep sage and forest greens of actual plants), warm amber and golden yellows of sunlight, earthy terracotta and ochre, sky blues. Never cold blues or grays.

Illustration style: organic curves, botanical motifs integrated with technological elements, community and human warmth always present.

Typography: warm and humanistic — slightly informal but readable, organic letterforms.

Apply this to my [product/campaign]: [describe what you're building — it should have environmental relevance]. The result should make people feel hopeful rather than guilty about the future — this is optimistic environmentalism, not catastrophism.
```
