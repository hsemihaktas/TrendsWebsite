## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [long-form article/magazine-style page/brand storytelling section] in Editorial Design style.

Editorial design is the art of making reading a visual experience. It borrows from over a century of magazine and newspaper layout craft, where every page is considered as both content and composition.

The headline does the heavy lifting: large, dramatic, occupying more space than you'd think reasonable. The relationship between headline, image, and body text is carefully choreographed — the eye moves through them in a deliberate sequence.

Grid: editorial design is rigorous about its grid but expresses personality through how it breaks it. Columns align to a baseline grid. Captions, pullquotes, and footnotes all have designated positions. The consistency creates the space for occasional, intentional breaks.

Photography is "art-directed" — it doesn't just illustrate the story, it IS part of it. Images are sized deliberately (a full-bleed photograph means something different than a small inline one).

Typography hierarchy: at least 4 distinct levels — display headline, section heading, body text, caption — each with clear visual differentiation.

Apply this to my [article/page/section] about [topic]. My content structure: [headline, subheads, body text, images/diagrams]. The result should make the reader feel they're reading something worth their full attention — not just scrolling.
```
