## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [wellness app/environmental product/health platform] using Biophilic Design principles.

Biophilic design is rooted in the concept of biophilia — humans' innate affinity for the natural world. Applied to digital interfaces, it uses the visual and structural patterns of nature to create spaces that feel restorative, calm, and inherently trustworthy.

The visual language draws from actual natural systems: the branching patterns of trees (dendritic navigation), the proportional spirals of shells (golden ratio compositions), the irregular organic shapes of leaves and stones (asymmetric but balanced layouts).

Color palette: directly from nature, not from a color wheel. Forest greens, warm earthy ochres, stone grays, sky blues, cream and linen whites. Avoid any color that doesn't exist in a natural environment.

Shapes: organic curves instead of geometric precision. Soft rounded forms that resemble river stones, leaves, and hillsides. Nothing sharp unless it's referencing something sharp in nature (crystal, thorn).

Texture: subtle natural textures add depth without decoration — paper grain, linen weave, stone surface. Applied at very low opacity as background layers.

Apply this to my [product/interface] for [user/use case]. The result should make users feel physically calmer — like briefly stepping outside from a screen-heavy day.
```
