Design a [personal brand/artisan shop/recipe site/lifestyle product] in Cottagecore aesthetic.

Cottagecore is a celebration of idealized rural life — the kind of slow, handmade, flower-filled existence that serves as the visual antidote to screen-heavy urban living. It romanticizes the domestic arts: baking bread, pressing flowers, tending a garden, hand-stitching.

The visual world: warm morning light filtering through linen curtains, wildflowers in a ceramic vase, a handwritten recipe, a worn wooden table covered in baked goods. Everything feels made by hand, grown in a garden, or inherited from a grandmother.

Botanical illustration: delicate line-drawn flowers, botanically accurate leaves, hand-painted watercolor-style imagery. Simple and precise, not whimsical.

Typography: a calligraphic or handwritten-looking font for display, a refined serif for body text. Feels like handwriting in a recipe book or a letter from a friend.

Apply this to my [brand/product]: [describe what you're creating — food, craft, lifestyle, wellness, etc.]. The result should make people feel like they've stepped outside the city into a slower, more beautiful world — even if they're looking at it on a screen.
