## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [educational platform/book subscription/writing app/private club] in Dark Academia aesthetic.

Dark Academia is a cultural aesthetic built around the romance of scholarship — the kind depicted in Dead Poets Society, The Secret History, and Donna Tartt's novels. It's the beauty of old libraries, candlelit study sessions, marble statues in dim galleries, Latin on the chalkboard.

The visual world: dark warm backgrounds (deep chocolate brown, midnight navy, aged black), illuminated by warm amber light (candles, brass lamps, firelight). Ancient wood, leather bindings, yellowed paper, classical sculpture.

Color palette: everything is dark and warm. Deep walnut browns, aged parchment creams, candlelight ambers, forest greens, burgundy wine. No bright whites, no cool blues, no neon. The entire palette exists in shadows and warm light.

Typography: serif always — a book-quality serif for body text, a more dramatic gothic or old-style serif for headlines. Dense text setting feels appropriate and intelligent, not overwhelming.

Decorative elements: classical motifs (laurel wreaths, Greek key patterns), vintage illustrations, botanical sketches, old maps, celestial charts.

Apply this to my [platform/product]: [describe what you're building and who it's for]. The result should feel like discovering a secret library — exclusive, intellectual, slightly melancholy, and deeply romantic about learning.
```
