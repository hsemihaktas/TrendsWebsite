## AI Prompt

Bu tasarım stilini yapay zekayla uygulamak için aşağıdaki prompt'u kopyalayın:

```text
Design a [dashboard/data tool/professional app] in Monochrome UI style.

Monochrome UI uses a single hue family — all elements derive from one base color, varying only in lightness and saturation. There is no secondary color, no accent, no complementary contrast. All hierarchy comes from tonal relationships within a single palette.

The discipline forces extreme precision: if the only tool for creating hierarchy is tonal contrast, every decision about lightness and saturation must be intentional. A 5% difference in lightness can be the entire difference between "active" and "inactive."

Color choice matters enormously: deep charcoal/black (the most sophisticated), navy blue (corporate authority), warm brown/tan (approachable and human), sage green (calm and focused). The base hue sets the emotional register of the entire interface.

Accent and CTA: in a monochrome system, the single CTA button might be the only element at full saturation — making it immediately magnetic without needing a contrasting color.

Text hierarchy: the same monochrome approach applies — headline at darkest tone, body at medium, helper text at lightest readable value.

Apply this to my [interface/product]: [describe what you're building]. My chosen hue family: [or describe the emotional quality you want — authoritative, calm, sophisticated, etc.]. The result should feel intentional and restrained — like every color decision was made consciously, not by default.
```
